My Story

Hey there! I’m Rakshith Suresh — a data enthusiast, problem-solver, and someone who genuinely gets excited about transforming raw data into meaningful insights.


Bit more about me

I’m a Data Analyst with hands-on experience across data engineering, business intelligence, and predictive analytics. After graduating with a Bachelor's in Computer Science from PES University, I began my professional journey at Blue Dart Express, where I built forecasting models and fraud detection logic that saved costs and improved logistics operations.

I then joined LTIMindtree as a Business Data Analyst, where I led end-to-end data initiatives — designing ELT pipelines using dbt and Snowflake, building KPI dashboards with Tableau and Power BI, and delivering insights that helped global clients enhance operational efficiency. My projects spanned customer segmentation, demand forecasting, and ad-hoc analytics for key stakeholders.

Today, I work as a Data Scientist at Bright Start Ed-Tech, leading initiatives that have improved student retention and optimized marketing ROI through machine learning and real-time dashboards. My stack includes SQL, Python, dbt, BigQuery, Fivetran, Power BI, and Tableau. I thrive on building scalable data solutions that solve real business problems — from pipeline to dashboard.
