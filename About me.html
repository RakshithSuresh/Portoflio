My Story

Hey there! I’m Rakshith Suresh — a data enthusiast, problem-solver, and someone who genuinely gets excited about transforming raw data into meaningful insights.


Bit more about me

My journey into the world of data began during my undergrad days at PES University, India, where I discovered the thrill of unraveling patterns hidden in massive datasets. That passion took shape into a full-fledged career, starting with an internship at Blue Dart Express. There, I dove into logistics data, built forecasting models, and even helped prevent fraud — all using Python and SQL. That experience taught me that good data can do more than inform — it can drive real business change. Post graduation, I joined LTIMindtree as a Business Data Analyst. I worked on everything from demand forecasting to customer segmentation and KPI dashboards. I got to build data pipelines and visualizations that helped execs make smarter decisions. It wasn’t just about crunching numbers — it was about crafting a story from data that stakeholders could actually use. Now, I’m in Los Angeles, pursuing my Master’s in Information Systems (Data Analytics) at California State University, Long Beach. Alongside my studies, I’m working as a Data Scientist at Bright Start Ed-Tech, where I’m using machine learning and analytics to improve customer retention and maximize marketing ROI. I’ve also built dashboards that saved hours of reporting time and helped leadership make faster, smarter decisions. My toolkit? SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, ML models, and a never-ending curiosity to explore new data challenges. Whether it’s forecasting EV trends in Washington or optimizing ad spend, I love using data to answer tough questions and make life just a little more efficient. When I’m not diving into datasets, you’ll probably find me tinkering with side projects, exploring new tech, or soaking in the sunny vibes of Southern California.

If you're interested in data analytics, data engineering, or share any of my interests, I'd love to connect. Feel free to reach out via email or connect with me on LinkedIn.

Let’s connect and see how data can power up your next big idea.
