My work directly contributed to measurable outcomes, including a 12% gain in operational efficiency.

I’m skilled in Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, and Snowflake, and have hands-on experience with tools like Airflow, Apache Kafka, and AWS. I specialize in turning complex datasets into actionable insights — from building scalable data pipelines to designing executive-level dashboards that drive strategic decisions.
